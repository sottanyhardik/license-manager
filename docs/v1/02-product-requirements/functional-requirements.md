# Functional requirements

V1 preserves, subject to contract validation: licence/item management; BOE/import evidence; allotment/allocation and planning; trade/invoice/payment evidence; BOE reconciliation; master data and users; reports/exports; document parsing; and operational jobs. Classifications: **IMPROVE** for all high-risk workflows; **UNDECIDED** for GL/bank reconciliation/SaaS because evidence does not establish them.
