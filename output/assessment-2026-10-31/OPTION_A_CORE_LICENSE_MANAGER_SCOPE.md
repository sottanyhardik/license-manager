# Option A - Core License Manager without new accounting

## Included

Reliable operational licence management: tenant/company separation; master governance; canonical licence/BOE/allotment/restriction calculations; DFIA/SION planning; 2/3/5/10% and AU rule decisions; BOE/invoice/trade/transfer workflows; operational reconciliation; data remediation; document/audit controls; owner dashboard; reports/exports; UI/UX; testing, performance, recovery and release controls.

## Explicit exclusions

No new chart of accounts, double-entry journals, vouchers, GL, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, AR/AP accounting ledgers, bank statement reconciliation, GST/TDS accounting, period close or Tally migration.

## Small-business operating model

Employee enters/imports a source record once; service validates master, licence, capacity and restrictions; owner sees only actionable exceptions (expiry, missing data, duplicate candidates, mismatches, failed imports and high-impact changes); the owner approves only merge/delete/reversal/override/large-import exceptions. Month-end is an operational completeness check, not an accounting close.

## Scope acceptance

Balances and restriction capacities agree across all interfaces; no cross-company records leak; no concurrent overspend; corrections are traceable/reversible where policy permits; reconciliation candidates are evidence-backed; owner dashboard drills into each alert.
