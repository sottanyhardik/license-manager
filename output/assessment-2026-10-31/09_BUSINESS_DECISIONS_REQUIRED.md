# Business decisions required

| Decision | Recommended default | Risk of postponing |
|---|---|---|
| Internal multi-company or external SaaS? | Internal scoped multi-company first | Unsafe/expensive tenant assumptions. |
| Restriction/AU bases, groups, priority, overrides | Written owner/DGFT evidence; block ambiguity | Incorrect utilisation. |
| Historical duplicate/remediation policy | Preserve and reverse/merge with evidence | Data loss/misstatement. |
| Employee vs owner approval thresholds | Only high-impact actions need owner review | Either unsafe changes or excessive friction. |
| Essential dashboard/reports | Daily exception queue + operational monthly check | Scope creep. |
| Accounting now or later? | Option A now unless cash/AR/AP need is urgent | Unfunded migration complexity. |
| Option B chart/tax/opening/bank format | CA-approved policy before design | Financial/legal rework. |
