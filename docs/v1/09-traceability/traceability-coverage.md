## Interpretation

The measurable denominators prevent hidden scope. They do not satisfy the audit’s completion criterion: “0” in the individual-contract row is an explicit implementation/audit backlog, not an absence of functionality. The 354-operation family map is useful evidence but cannot substitute for per-operation DTO, policy, consumer and regression mapping.
